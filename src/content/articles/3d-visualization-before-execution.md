# Why 3D Spatial Planning Must Precede Execution: The Science of Error-Free Layouts

One of the most expensive mistakes in interior design is discovering on-site that a wardrobe obstructs a bedroom window, or that a kitchen island leaves inadequate clearance for opening the refrigerator.

At **Radhika Mahajan Architects**, we enforce a strict operating principle: **Never cut a single sheet of plywood until the client has walked through a 100% photorealistic 3D digital model.**

---

## What True 3D Visualization Solves

### 1. Accurate Ergonomics & Clearance
A 2D blueprint cannot convey how narrow a 3-foot corridor feels when lined with full-height wardrobes. 3D spatial models simulate exact physical clearances, ensuring you never bump into door handles or sharp edges.

### 2. Lighting & Shadow Simulation
Light changes throughout the day. Using architectural rendering engines, we simulate:
- Natural morning and evening sun entering your specific floor and orientation in Pune.
- The warm 2700K ambient glow of recessed ceiling coves.
- Accent spotlights highlighting artwork and textured stone walls.

### 3. Material Coordination in Real Contrast
Selecting a laminate swatch in a showroom is misleading. In 3D rendering, you see how your chosen fluted oak veneer interacts with warm beige wall paint, matte black handles, and gray porcelain tiles under real lighting conditions.

---

## Eliminating Costly Rework

When clients approve a verified 3D model, change requests during construction drop by over 95%. This protects your project budget from mid-project variation bills and prevents timeline delays.

Experience your home in full dimensional clarity before execution begins.
