# The Architecture of Budget Transparency: How Detailed BOQs Prevent Hidden Costs

One of the most frequent grievances homeowners express when discussing past interior renovations is the dreaded **budget escalation**. A project initially quoted at ₹12 Lakh ends up costing ₹18 Lakh due to "unforeseen extras" and vague initial estimates.

At **Radhika Mahajan Architects**, we eliminate financial ambiguity through exhaustive **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)** planning.

---

## Why Lump-Sum Quotes Are a Red Flag

When an interior contractor provides a vague one-page quotation with broad figures like *"Kitchen: ₹2,50,000"* or *"False Ceiling: ₹1,20,000"*, they leave critical specifications undefined:
- What plywood brand will be used?
- Is electrical wiring, conduit, and switch replacement included?
- Does the false ceiling price include perimeter cove lighting channels and multi-coat putty?
- Who pays for transportation, debris removal, and deep cleaning?

When these items inevitably arise on site, the contractor charges them as "extra work".

---

## The RMA Detailed Line-Item BOQ

Our quotations specify every single material and labor component in clear line items:
1. **Material Specifications**: Exact brand names (Century / Greenply / Hettich / Asian Paints).
2. **Dimension Measurements**: Square-footage and running-footage verified against 3D drawings.
3. **Hardware Grades**: Specific model numbers for soft-close telescopic channels, hydraulic lift-ups, and profile handles.
4. **Comprehensive Scope**: Electrical cabling, painting, civil modifications, and post-completion deep cleaning are accounted for up-front.

---

## The 40-40-20 Milestone Structure

We link financial commitments to tangible physical progress:
- **40% Advance**: Material procurement, carcass board cutting, and civil mobilization.
- **40% Mid-Execution**: On-site carcass installation, false ceiling completion, and laminate pressing.
- **20% Handover**: Final painting, electrical lighting fit-out, snag list closure, and official key handover.

No hidden surprises. Absolute peace of mind.
