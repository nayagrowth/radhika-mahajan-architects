# The 45-Day Execution Blueprint: Eliminating Delay and Chaos in Interior Construction

For most homeowners taking possession of a new 3 or 4 BHK flat in Pune, interior work is often synonymous with anxiety: endless delays, carpenter absenteeism, unexpected cost escalations, and unfulfilled promises.

At **Radhika Mahajan Architects**, we believe that execution is an engineered process, not an unpredictable gamble. Our **45-Day Turnkey Blueprint** was developed to provide clients with absolute timeline certainty.

---

## Phase 1: Days 1–10 — Civil, Demolition & Electrical Routing

Before aesthetic finishes begin, the core infrastructure must be locked:
1. **Civil Modifications**: Non-structural wall adjustments and niche creation.
2. **Electrical Conduit Routing**: Wiring for automation, ambient 3000K LED coves, AC conduits, and two-way bedside switches.
3. **Plumbing Modifications**: Sanitary line alignments and concealed diverters.
4. **Site Protection**: Flooring masking with heavy-duty protective sheets.

---

## Phase 2: Days 11–25 — False Ceiling & Carcass Carpentry

With infrastructure validated, the carpentry and ceiling teams mobilize in parallel:
- **False Ceiling Framework**: Heavy-gauge GI framework with moisture-resistant gypsum boards.
- **Modular Carcass Fabrication**: Calibrated BWR plywood carcasses cut with millimeter accuracy for wardrobes, kitchen base units, and TV consoles.
- **Hardware Dry-Fitting**: Hettich soft-close channels and concealed hinges fitted and tested for alignment.

---

## Phase 3: Days 26–38 — Surface Finishes, Veneers & Laminates

The character of the space emerges:
- **Laminate & Veneer Pressing**: Bubble-free hydraulic pressing with zero edge peeling.
- **Countertop & Italian Marble Installation**: Precision water-jet cutting and polishing.
- **First-Coat Putty & Primer**: Multi-coat surface preparation on walls and ceilings.

---

## Phase 4: Days 39–45 — Lighting, Deep Cleaning & Snagging Handover

The final reveal:
- **Architectural Lighting Fixture Fitment**: Magnetic tracks, recessed downlights, and decorative pendants.
- **Final Top-Coat Asian Paints Royale**: Seamless matte finish.
- **Deep Industrial Cleaning & Quality Audit**: 40-point principal architect inspection before keys are handed over.

---

## Transparent Accountability: The 3-Day Update Rule

Throughout the 45-day cycle, clients receive photographic progress logs and milestone checklists on WhatsApp every 3 days. No ambiguity. No guesswork. Just predictable, joyful handover.
