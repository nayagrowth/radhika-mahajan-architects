# Material Integrity: The Engineering Behind Calibrated Plywood, Moisture Resistance, and German Joinery

The true value of an interior project is hidden behind the laminates and wall paint. What lies beneath your kitchen counters and wardrobe shutters determines whether your interiors look pristine for 15 years or begin sagging within 18 months.

At **Radhika Mahajan Architects**, we maintain an uncompromising standard for core structural materials and mechanical joinery.

---

## 1. Plywood: Calibrated BWR vs. Cheap Commercial Boards

Many contractors cut costs by using commercial MR (Moisture Resistant) plywood or uncalibrated particle board in wet areas.

### Our Standard:
- **Kitchen & Bathrooms**: 100% Boiling Water Resistant (BWR) / BWP Marine-grade plywood (Century / Greenply), treated with anti-termite and anti-borer chemicals.
- **Wardrobes & Bed Frames**: Calibrated Hardwood Plywood with uniform thickness across all sheets, preventing surface waviness and uneven laminate bonding.

---

## 2. Hardware: The Precision of Hettich & Ebco

A wardrobe shutter is opened thousands of times over its lifespan. Poor hinges result in drooping doors, squeaks, and misalignment.

We exclusively install:
- **Soft-Close Concealed Hinges**: Tested for 100,000 opening cycles.
- **Tandem Box Drawers**: Heavy-duty 30kg–50kg load-bearing capacity with smooth hydraulic glide.
- **Profile Handles & Concealed Channels**: Anodized aluminum profiles that resist oxidation in humid climates.

---

## 3. Surface Finishes: Zero Toxic Off-Gassing

We prioritize indoor air quality and healthy living environments:
- **Low-VOC Adhesives**: Eliminating sharp chemical odors during and after installation.
- **Asian Paints Royale Emulsion**: Anti-bacterial, washable, and lead-free wall finishes.
- **Natural Stones & Quartz**: Heat-resistant, non-porous kitchen countertops that resist turmeric and oil stains.
